# Commercial Licensing (Template)

This is a starting point for commercial terms, not a signed agreement. Have a lawyer review the final EULA.

## Suggested license families

| License | Typical use | Suggested scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Indie** | Small teams / one commercial title | One product, revenue cap, no source redistribution |
| **Studio** | Multiple titles / larger teams | Multiple products, named entities, limited seats |
| **Source** | Custom engine work | Source access, modification rights, no sublicensing |
| **OEM** | Engine embedded in another product/engine | Broad distribution rights, negotiated royalty |

## Terms to define

- Licensee legal entity and named affiliates.
- Products covered, platforms, release dates.
- Whether source code is included, and who may access it.
- Seat count and contractor access.
- Rights to modify, fork and redistribute.
- Royalty or flat fee, payment schedule, audits.
- Support, updates, and maintenance window.
- Ownership of modifications and contributions.
- Trademark/branding rights for "V.O.I.D." and "PHYSICS LAB".
- Confidentiality and source escrow (optional).
- Warranties, indemnity and liability limits.
- Termination and post-termination obligations.
- Third-party notices and compliance (Box3D, SDL3, GLM, volk, Vulkan-Headers, stb, Roboto, DXC, MOSS-SoundEffect-generated clips).
- Governing law and jurisdiction.
